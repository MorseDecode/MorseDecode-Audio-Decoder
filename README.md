## 🛠 What makes MorseDecode special?
Based on our core technology:

* **Adaptive Audio Decoding**: Seamlessly converts WAV, MP3, and OGG into text.
* **Real-time Visualization**: Watch signal waveforms and detect WPM (Words Per Minute) on the fly.
* **Privacy-First**: No data uploads; all decoding happens locally in your browser.
* **Global Support**: Designed for ham radio operators and learners worldwide.
